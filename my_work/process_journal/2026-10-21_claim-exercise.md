# Claim exercise on the coexistence proposal

## What I set out to do

Use the five-slide proposal, *A Photograph of Coexisting Memories: Through Narrative-Driven Computational Imaging*, as the inspectable output for the claim exercise. Write an initial four-sentence claim and check it against the text of Slides 2, 4, and 5.

## What I expected to happen

The draft journal does not record an expectation.

## What actually happened

The main observation was conceptual rather than experimental: generating alternative imaging branches and placing them together within one photograph are two different design operations. The claim was revised so it does not imply that the photographic method has already been implemented or tested.

## The gap

Version 1 could be read as a result of making images. The slides only separate proposed stages. Version 3 keeps the design distinction and drops the stronger wording that coexistence cannot be inferred from multiple branches.

## Numbers

Five slides. Three proposed stages: C1 narrative-to-imaging rules, C2 independent branching, C3 spatial coexistence. Three predefined recollection scenarios and three annotated prints planned by 25 November 2026. Three dated claim versions kept in `my_work/claim.md`.

## Alternatives I rejected, and why
