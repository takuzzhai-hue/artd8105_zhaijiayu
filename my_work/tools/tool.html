Version 3 — 指令 / instruction

The figure is an hourglass-shaped structure made of disconnected individual lines. The lines gradually increase in length and then decrease again, with each line rotating 5 degrees to the right. Two sets of parallel thin lines converge toward the center from above and below, forming a narrow, interwoven structure in the middle, and then expand outward in opposite directions. The visual structure consists of two groups of straight lines arranged into two opposing fan shapes. The lines in the upper section extend upward and gradually spread apart, forming an open fan shape. The overall outline resembles an elongated hourglass, a twisted ribbon, or two sets of parallel lines interweaving with each other. Version 3: the middle is a twist (rope/cloth from one face to the other), not a point. The two fans do not join at a vertex.

穿过腰部的直线条数。同一条线的两端分别形成上扇和下扇。默认 30。

「each line rotating 5 degrees to the right」→ 相对前一条累加，顺时针。

「twisted ribbon」+ 你给的图：腰点从左排到右，线彼此交错。0 会回到交于一点。72 mm 是为了能看出扭转，请改到你要的宽度。

整束相对竖直的旋转。每条仍相对前一条顺时针累加夹角。

同一 seed 永远同一张图。当前几何由参数完全决定，seed 不改变线条；记下它是为了墙签和以后若你打开随机。
