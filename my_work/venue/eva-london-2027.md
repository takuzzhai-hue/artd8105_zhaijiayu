### Call for papers — key passages, verbatim

> Please submit a summary proposal of up to 400 words in text form. For full (i.e. more than 2 pages) paper proposals only, we require a draft of the paper as a PDF.

> Papers accepted for EVA London 2027 will be published both in the bound colour proceedings (with ISBN and ISSN 1477-9358) that delegates can order and also in the online open-access proceedings (with individual DOIs for each paper).
