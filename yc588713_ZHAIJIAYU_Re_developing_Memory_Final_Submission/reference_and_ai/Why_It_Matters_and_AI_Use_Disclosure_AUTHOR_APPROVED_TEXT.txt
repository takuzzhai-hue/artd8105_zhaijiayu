WHY IT MATTERS
Photographs usually appear as finished outputs, concealing the computational choices through which they become visible. Re-developing Memory turns those choices into artistic material and connects them to personal recollection. By distinguishing RAW-derived renderings, non-generative photographic interventions, and AI-generated additions, the work makes image provenance part of its visual language. Its staged, strip-based composition invites the SIGGRAPH community to reconsider photographic finality, the expressive potential of imaging pipelines, and the ethical boundary between recorded evidence and computational imagination.

AI USE DISCLOSURE

AI WITHIN THE ARTWORK
Re-developing Memory combines AI-assisted analysis with conventional image processing and, only when authorized by the user's recollection or request, constrained image generation. The current implementation uses the following services and models:

- Alibaba Cloud Model Studio (Bailian / DashScope), Qwen Plus (qwen-plus): interprets Chinese, English, and mixed-language recollections, identifies photographic intentions, and translates them into structured operation plans within the project's defined operation catalogue and parameter limits.
- Alibaba Cloud Model Studio, Qwen3-VL-Plus (qwen3-vl-plus): examines the uploaded photograph, identifies visible objects and candidate regions, assists in deciding whether a requested addition is absent, and reviews proposed or generated additions and spatial extensions for consistency with the request and source photograph. These checks are fallible and do not establish historical truth.
- Alibaba Cloud Model Studio, Qwen Image 2.0 Pro (qwen-image-2.0-pro): supplies constrained photographic additions and same-scene spatial extensions when the corresponding generation pathway is authorized. Additions are limited to explicitly requested objects or concrete objects recalled as having been present, subject to visual checks; a wish or metaphor alone does not authorize adding an object. Generated content is intended to remain consistent with the source photograph's lighting and texture. Newly imagined faces are represented through non-identifying white, blurred shapes rather than reconstructed facial identities. Such shapes can be produced by local compositing rather than by the image-generation model.
- Meta Segment Anything Model 2.1, Hiera Tiny (facebook/sam2.1-hiera-tiny): runs locally to estimate masks for selected objects and regions. It determines where an operation is applied; it does not generate replacement scene content. The implementation pins model revision de431c4043854a71d8101e17995dfe596bf101a5.
- Depth Anything V2 Small (depth-anything/Depth-Anything-V2-Small-hf): runs locally to estimate relative depth for synthetic, depth-guided defocus. It does not recover the original focus plane or lost detail. The implementation pins model revision 5426e4f0f36572d16453bbda7a8389317b1bef99. When depth estimation is unavailable, the system can use a separately recorded mask-based blur fallback.

The Qwen identifiers above describe the current configured model or code defaults, not immutable model versions for every historical output. Available execution records should be used to identify the model actually used for a particular example. Not every model is invoked for every photograph.

PROCESSING AND PROVENANCE
The project distinguishes RAW-derived rendering (R), other non-generative interventions (E), and generated supplementation (G). RAW decoding and development use rawpy/LibRaw; compositing and effects use conventional image-processing libraries including NumPy, OpenCV, and Pillow. These libraries are not themselves generative AI. AI-assisted segmentation or depth estimation does not make the resulting blur, silhouette, exposure adjustment, or compositing a generated scene. Surface damage, cut-outs, touch traces, fragment rearrangement, and image-to-code effects are computational simulations, not evidence of physical damage, real fingerprints, or recovered memories. Mixed processing is recorded where applicable.

JPEG and PNG examples cannot demonstrate access to original RAW sensor measurements. Examples produced from those formats are described as display-image processing, even when their appearance is inspired by photographic development. Stage records distinguish generated additions from non-generative changes and retain the associated recollection and operation information; generation records include prompts, model identifiers, review results, and seeds where available. AI-generated additions and outpainted regions are speculative supplements, not photographic evidence of remembered events. Automated review reduces some errors but does not guarantee authenticity or accuracy.

AI ASSISTANCE IN DEVELOPMENT AND SUBMISSION PREPARATION
OpenAI Codex and Cursor were used as AI-assisted programming environments during development. Assistance included project scaffolding, implementation and revision of the processing pipeline and web interface, API integration, semantic mapping and prompt development, testing, and debugging. Codex also assisted with preparing demonstration inputs, running image-processing examples, and organizing their output records. The underlying model versions used across all Codex and Cursor sessions were not consistently recorded and are not asserted here.

ChatGPT (OpenAI) assisted with English translation, manuscript wording and organization, layout preparation, preliminary reference checking, and drafting the AI-use disclosure. AI-assisted reference checks are not treated as independent verification of bibliographic or art-historical claims. Some demonstration recollections were drafted with AI specifically to exercise the system's effects; these must be identified as demonstration text rather than participant testimony or collected research data.

The artist established and iteratively revised the artistic aims, acceptable effects, semantic mappings, constraints on generation, and presentation format. AI assistance does not substitute for the author's responsibility to review the submitted text, verify references and technical claims, identify the provenance of each illustrated example, and approve the final selection of images. This disclosure describes the documented tools and current implementation; it does not imply that all illustrated outputs contain AI-generated visual content.
