# Reference verification report

This audit checks whether each of the 16 bibliography entries can be identified. It does not check every interpretation in the paper. The English bibliography keeps all 16 entries. Corrections were limited to citation details. "Verified" means the work was identified, not that every sentence in the paper follows from it.

## Results

1. Bergson (1991 [1896]). Verified. Zone Books, 1991, translated by Paul and Palmer. An in-text 1911 date in the Chinese paper was aligned to this 1991 edition. https://eclass.uoa.gr/modules/document/file.php/PSPA254/BergsonMatterAndMemory.pdf
2. Brown (2023). Verified. Wiley chapter, pp. 81–98. https://doi.org/10.1002/9781119827214.ch5
3. Domestic Data Streamers (2023). Verified. The project page dates Synthetic Memories to 2023. It is not cited in the current body text. https://www.domesticstreamers.com/work/synthetic-memories/
4. Flusser (2000 [1983]). Verified. Reaktion, 2000, translated by Anthony Mathews. https://reaktionbooks.co.uk/work/towards-a-philosophy-of-photography
5. Karaimer and Brown (2016). Verified. ECCV 2016, pp. 429–444. DOI 10.1007/978-3-319-46448-0_26. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-46448-0_26
6. Kitasono (1966). Verified. *A Note on Plastic Poetry*, VOU no. 104, February/March 1966, p. 23. https://www.kitasonokatue.com/new-page-4
7. Kganye (2016). Verified, with a limit. The Chazen Museum record identifies the artist, the series, the year, and one photograph. It does not by itself document the whole series. https://chazen.wisc.edu/collection/29751/re-palame-tereneng-e-fosahetseng-from-the/
8. Mavropoulou (2021–2023). Verified. The artist site confirms the title, the dates, and the AI-generated family album. https://www.mariamavropoulou.com/imagined-images
9. Mayer (1971). Verified. Siglio describes the 1971 activity, the 1972 installation, and the 2020 republication. https://sigliopress.com/readings/memory-mayer-history/
10. Mays (2011). Verified. The artist site and a Museum of Contemporary Photography record confirm the series. Opening one catalog link can return a 403 error. https://www.aspenmays.com/
11. Müller-Pohle (1995–1998). Verified. The project page gives the years and the alphanumeric transcription. https://muellerpohle.net/projects/digital-scores/
12. Pataranutaporn et al. (2025). Verified. CHI 2025, DOI 10.1145/3706598.3713697. https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3713697
13. rawpy API. Verified. The page is undated, so the citation is n.d., accessed 24 November 2026. https://letmaik.github.io/rawpy/api/rawpy.Params.html
14. Ruff (2004–2007). Verified, with a limit. The MoMA record is one 2004 object, *jpeg msh01*, inside the *jpegs* series. It does not document every year of the series. https://www.moma.org/collection/works/149384
15. Wade et al. (2002). Verified. *Psychonomic Bulletin & Review* 9(3), 597–603. DOI 10.3758/BF03196318. https://doi.org/10.3758/BF03196318
16. Zhang, Cheng, and Luo (2024). Verified. PACMCGIT 7(4), article 51. DOI 10.1145/3664207. https://doi.org/10.1145/3664207
