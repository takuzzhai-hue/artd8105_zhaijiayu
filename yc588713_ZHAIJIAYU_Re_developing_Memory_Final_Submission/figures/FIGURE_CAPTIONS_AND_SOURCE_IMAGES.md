# Artwork figures and source panels — English captions

The numbered figure files are composites used in the manuscript. The `source_panels/` directory preserves the exact fifteen image files supplied by the author, renamed into English for organization **without visual reprocessing**. Filenames indicate the reported case and order; they do not independently validate which model or algorithm produced each image. Compare all stages against authentic execution logs before asserting their full provenance.

## Figure 1. Pendulum: time within an instant

(a) Source photograph; (b–e) cumulative adjustments reported as global exposure −0.35 EV, local light-ring exposure −0.42 EV, localized motion blur on the pendulum sphere (amount 0.11; angle 0°), and vignette (amount 0.12); (f) final vertical-strip composite. Stage mapping and parameter logs should be independently verified.

## Figure 2. Vanishing hand: the shape of forgetting

Source photograph and a local whiteout result. White remains in the space formerly occupied by the hand and ring rather than seamlessly reconstructing the background. The manuscript describes three cumulative stages, but the three intermediate output files were not all supplied.

## Figure 3. Adding an index finger: touch beyond the frame

Source Chicago waterfront photograph and a result with a foreground pointing gesture. The finger did not appear in the original frame. This is speculative generation, not direct photographic evidence of the recollected moment.

## Figure 4. Forest: spatial rupture beyond framing

Source photograph, three visible process stages and a composite containing displacement, stretching, repeated forest segments, and visible hard seams. Original-frame and outpaint boundaries require comparison with authentic stage logs.

## Rights and privacy note

Before uploading `source_panels/` to a public GitHub repository, check camera metadata, personal content, consent to exhibit each photograph, and permission to share any associated recollection. The package does not establish such permission.
